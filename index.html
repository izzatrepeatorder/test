2 · Where the 4-category bills break down

Half of all misses are a single category away from a 4-category bill. Of 14,130 bills below the 4-category bar, 7,312 (52%) already have 3 of 4. Among bills that contain a main course, the missing leg is almost always the starter or the side — not the drink:

4 · Non-obvious insights (bill-value lift)

Roasted Bone Marrow effect. Bills with Bone Marrow average RM660 vs RM384 without — a +RM276 lift across 1,911 bills.
Starter lift. Any starter/soup lifts the bill to RM493 vs RM305 (+RM188).
Dessert lift. Dessert bills average RM492 vs RM360 (+RM132).
Pasta-only pool. 2,974 bills are pasta-without-steak, averaging RM225 vs RM539 for steak/lobster bills — a RM314/bill trade-up opportunity.
